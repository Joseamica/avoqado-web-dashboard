## Design Context

### Users

Avoqado is used by restaurant and retail operators: cashiers closing a busy shift, venue managers
reviewing controls, and owners or supervisors configuring operations. Many are not technical and
often work under time pressure. The interface must make the next safe action obvious without a
manual, especially when handling money.

### Brand Personality

Clear, dependable, and operational. The product should create calm confidence and a sense of
control, using direct language and honest states rather than decorative excitement.

### Aesthetic Direction

Refined utilitarian clarity, informed by Square and Toast operational workflows and integrated into
Avoqado's existing semantic-token design system. Support light and dark themes. Avoid flashy
fintech styling, decorative gradients, excessive glassmorphism, generic metric-card grids, and any
visual treatment that makes financial status ambiguous.

### Design Principles

1. Design for the least technical operator and make the safe next action immediately legible.
2. Treat money states as evidence: distinguish zero, missing, balanced, short, and over explicitly.
3. Preserve existing information hierarchy and progressively disclose additive capabilities.
4. Use semantic tokens, established components, and concise i18n copy in every supported language.
5. Meet WCAG AA, retain visible keyboard focus, and never rely on color alone to communicate state.
